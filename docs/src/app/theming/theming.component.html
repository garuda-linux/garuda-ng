Theming

How it works

Since GarudaNG is built on top of PrimeNG, we also make use of its theming system. GarudaNG comes with a variety of pre-built themes that can be used out of the box. These themes are available in a separate package called @garudalinux/themes.

Custom Themes

You can also create your own custom themes by following the guidelines provided by PrimeNG in their theming documentation. Once you have created your custom theme, you can include it in your project and configure GarudaNG to use it.
